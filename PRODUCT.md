# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary Audience**: Ambitious small businesses, boutique service companies, private founders, and independent operators (hospitality venues, fashion ateliers, fitness facilities, and professional corporate advisory practices) who have outgrown generic website builders or neglected legacy sites.
- **User Situation**: Competing against larger or better-funded competitors, losing qualified inbound inquiries or bookings due to uninspiring, slow, or template-cluttered websites, and seeking an authoritative digital flagship that commands respect.
- **Job to Be Done**: Establish immediate digital credibility, communicate service offerings with high-fidelity clarity, provide effortless conversion funnels (consultation inquiries, table reservations, membership claims, project briefings), and avoid agency overhead.

## Product Purpose

- The A&H Devlo Studio platform is the official digital flagship, case study portfolio, and client acquisition engine for an independent web engineering and design practice.
- It demonstrates senior-tier engineering craft, bespoke visual design standards, and transparent collaboration to prospective clients.
- Success is measured by converting visiting founders and decision-makers into high-intent project inquiries with zero friction.

## Positioning

- **Core Mechanism**: Direct senior founder-engineer collaboration. Clients work directly with the designers and engineers building the site, with zero account managers, communication silos, or junior delegate handoffs.
- **Performance Standard**: Sub-second Core Web Vitals, 100% bespoke production code (no generic off-the-shelf themes), and fluid responsive behavior across all viewports.
- **Commercial Transparency**: Transparent flat investment with milestone deliveries, providing total cost predictability.
- **True Ownership**: 100% client ownership of all code, assets, and design files upon completion, with zero proprietary platform lock-in.

## Operating Context

- **Client Evaluation Context**: Founders and business owners evaluating digital partners across desktop and mobile devices; frequently browsing portfolios on mobile devices outside of standard office hours.
- **Engagement Funnel**: Fast discovery → interactive case study inspection → transparent service and workflow review → multi-step project briefing modal or direct WhatsApp consultation.
- **Technical Environment**: React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Motion (Framer Motion), Vite, and semantic HTML5.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Confirmed Capabilities**:
  - Interactive Selected Work showcase with structured deep-dives (challenge, solution, deliverables, and conversion context).
  - Transparent service breakdown (Full Studio Websites, High-Converting Landing Pages, E-Commerce & Web Applications, Performance & Technical Audits).
  - Four-stage structured delivery workflow (Discover & Blueprint, Design & Architecture, Engineering & Performance, Launch & Evolution).
  - Multi-step interactive client inquiry modal with budget tiers, timeline selection, and service selection.
  - Zero-clipping responsive viewport architecture across Mobile (375px+), Tablet (768px+), Laptop (1366px–1440px), and Ultra-wide Desktop (1920px).
- **Technical & Identity Constraints**:
  - Strict preservation of the signature atmospheric navy & cyan identity (`#00141F`, `#021F33`, `#0B3B61`, `#2F7BFF`, `#E6FBFF`).
  - Fluid auto heights with balanced vertical padding; no rigid `100vh` locks that cause content clipping.
  - Natural document flow with single scroll container (zero nested scroll traps).

## Product Principles

- **1. Direct Senior Craftsmanship**: Every interaction, line of code, and visual detail is authored directly by senior builders—ensuring zero dilution from strategy to production.
- **2. Respect Visitor Time**: Zero friction, sub-second load times, instant clarity of services, and upfront pricing/process transparency.
- **3. Complete Ownership & Integrity**: Clients retain complete ownership of their digital assets and source code, free of vendor lock-in or obfuscation.
- **4. Functional Elegance**: Visual aesthetic must always serve readability, usability, and conversion—never sacrificing legibility for ornamental fluff.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA color contrast maintained across all dark navy surfaces against icy cyan headings and crisp body text.
- Semantic HTML landmarks throughout (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<button>`, `<a>`, `<form>`).
- Full keyboard focusability with visible focus indicators.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for animated transitions.
